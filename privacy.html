Privacy Policy

Clarity Photo · Last updated 17 August 2026

Clarity Photo collects no data. It has no analytics, no accounts, no trackers, and no servers. Your photos are enhanced entirely on your iPhone and never leave it.

Your photos

Every photo you enhance is processed on your device with Apple's Core ML, running the models bundled inside the app. Your photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or copied anywhere outside your device. The app makes no network calls of its own.

Results and their details are saved in the app's History so you can compare, re-save, or share them again. History lives in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting an item from History, clearing it in Settings, or deleting the app removes it permanently. We never receive a copy.

Photo library access

Clarity Photo asks for permission to add photos to your library, which is what saving an enhanced copy needs. Photos you enhance are chosen by you through Apple's photo picker or shared into the app; the app does not browse or read your library on its own.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs, and no code that connects to a network. There is no account to create and no personal information to give us. We could not identify a single user of this app.

Photo details

When you save or share an enhanced photo, Clarity Photo carries the original's capture date, camera details, and location across so the copy files alongside the original in your library. That information moves from one file to another on your device — it is never read by, sent to, or stored by the developer.

Extensions

Clarity Photo's share extension and its Photos editing extension run the same on-device enhancement, under the same rules. The share extension hands the photo to the app through a private container shared only between them; nothing is sent anywhere.

Purchases

The one-time Lifetime Unlock is handled entirely by Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether the unlock is active. Your payment details are handled by Apple and are never seen by, or shared with, the developer. Apple's handling of that transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing photos yourself

If you choose to share an enhanced photo using the iOS share sheet, that file goes wherever you send it — Messages, Mail, Files, or another app. That is your action, under your control, and this policy no longer applies once the photo leaves the app.

Children

Clarity Photo is safe for all ages. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date. Since the app collects no data, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a new practice.
